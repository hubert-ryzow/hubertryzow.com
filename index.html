SMP

I play on a Minecraft server with two friends. SMP is an iPhone app that keeps our farms, coordinates and enchants in one place.

Native SwiftUI, built in Xcode. The server runs Fabric with squaremap, which feeds the live map. There are no accounts or passwords, you just pick your profile.

I'm weighing an App Store release with a setup flow that works for any server.
